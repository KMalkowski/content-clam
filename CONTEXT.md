# Content Clam

Help viewers avoid unwanted videos while keeping control over what they see.

## Language

**Category**:
A defined kind of content or presentation that a video may match. Matching a category does not by itself mean the video should be filtered.

**Filter**:
A user's choice to dim videos matching a category. The viewer can edit the definition used to identify a match.
_Avoid_: Ban, deletion

**Dimming**:
A reversible visual treatment that marks a video as matching a filter and gives a reason. The viewer can reveal the video.

**Regretted click**:
A choice to open a video that the viewer later considers a poor use of their time.

**Credits**:
A prepaid balance used to pay for feed curation. One credit buys one successfully completed video analysis within the service's published limits.

**Hide Shorts**:
A viewer's choice to remove every Shorts shelf, card, and menu entry without analysis. Content already on screen is blurred rather than removed so the page does not jump.

**Allowed channel**:
A channel the viewer has chosen to exempt from filtering.

**Allowed topic**:
A subject the viewer has chosen to exempt from every category filter. A video matches when its main subject fits the viewer's description, rather than merely mentioning a keyword.

**Video analysis**:
An assessment of available video metadata against the viewer's enabled category definitions and topic exceptions.

**Personal API key**:
A viewer's own Jev credential, used to fund classification through their TypeSafe AI account instead of purchased extension credits.

**Trial allowance**:
A limited number of video analyses offered after email verification, before the viewer buys credits or supplies a personal API key.

**Saved classification**:
A video analysis result kept on the device for reuse while its metadata and rule definitions remain valid. It does not follow the viewer to other devices.

**Billing receipt**:
A temporary record of a credit operation used to prevent repeated charges. It contains no video information or classification result.

**Charged analyses**:
The total number of successful video analyses paid from a viewer's credit balance. Retries of an already charged operation do not increase this total.
